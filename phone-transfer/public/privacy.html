Phone transfer privacy

Prepared 6 November 2026. This page describes the optional phone playlist transfer.

What is transferred

Firebase Hosting serves this page. Firebase Anonymous Authentication creates a technical anonymous identity, without a signup screen. Firebase Realtime Database temporarily holds the pairing identifiers, identity IDs, timestamps, transfer status and the playlist URL or file text and optional name you send.

Connection and access

The page communicates with Firebase over HTTPS. Transfer access is restricted to the paired phone and car until the five-minute session expires. This is not end-to-end encryption: Firebase processes and stores the transfer. Firebase also receives ordinary connection information, such as your IP address.

Deletion and browser storage

The car attempts to delete the temporary transfer after a successful import. Cancellation and returning clients also attempt cleanup. Expiry denies session access but does not schedule physical deletion. If both clients disappear, abandoned data has no guaranteed deletion time. A deletion failure is shown as cleanup pending.

Your browser stores an anonymous authentication refresh credential and up to 16 known session IDs and deadlines so it can retry cleanup when you return. It does not persist playlist contents or the pairing secret in browser storage. Clearing browser storage removes that browser's cleanup identity. Anonymous authentication records are separate from transfer records; no automatic deletion of those identities is promised. Contact the publisher for privacy requests.

Your playlist

An uploaded file becomes a local copy saved in the car after your confirmation. Later phone file edits do not refresh that copy. A hosted playlist URL retains normal refresh behavior. Sending does not start playback. No audio passes through this transfer service.

No analytics or listening history

This page has no advertising or analytics SDK and does not collect listening history. Avoid transferring passwords, private access tokens or other sensitive information in playlists.
